About Pop'y Crunch

Pop'y Crunch is a local snack brand offering crispy and thin mini popia in two signature flavours: Cheese and Salted Egg. We aim to provide an affordable and convenient snack that brings a satisfying crunch to every bite.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, delicious and convenient snacks for everyone.

Our Vision

To grow Pop'y Crunch into a recognizable local snack brand.

Our Product

Crispy mini popia available in Cheese and Salted Egg flavours.

What Makes Us Different

  • Made with quality ingredients
  • Affordable pricing for everyone
  • Perfect for any occasion
  • Local Malaysian brand
